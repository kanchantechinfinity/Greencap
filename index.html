Licensed Pharmacy Partners — Monthly Lab Reports Included

Green Cap Health works exclusively with state-licensed compounding pharmacies that comply with applicable state and federal regulations. But compliance alone isn't enough — we require our pharmacy partners to run independent, third-party testing on every batch they produce for us. Those lab reports come directly to us. We review them. And they're available to you on request.

Because for an injectable medication, potency, sterility, pH, and endotoxin levels aren't optional details — they're the difference between a medication that works and one that causes harm. You should never be guessing what's in the vial.
